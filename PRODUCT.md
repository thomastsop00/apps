# Product Context

## Product
Thomas Tsopanakis Apps — a marketing website showcasing a portfolio of 6 iOS apps built by an independent Apple developer.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Potential app users browsing the App Store or searching for utility apps
- Existing users looking for support, privacy info, or other apps by the same developer
- Anyone discovering the developer's portfolio via search or social media

## Brand Voice
Clean, professional, approachable. The developer builds practical, well-designed utility apps. The site should feel modern and trustworthy, like Apple's own product pages but personal.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered app aggregator sites with ads everywhere
- Generic template sites with stock photography
- Over-animated SaaS landing pages with floating 3D elements
- Dark mysterious developer portfolios (this is friendly and accessible)

## Strategic Principles
- Let the apps speak for themselves — screenshots and icons are the stars
- Simple navigation: homepage grid → app detail → back
- Trust signals through clean design, not through badges or testimonials walls
- Mobile-first: visitors may be on their phones looking at App Store links
