Features

150+ Category Icons

Personalize each list with a unique icon. Find the perfect one for shopping, work, travel, or anything else.

Smart Sorting

Sort alphabetically, by date created, by date updated, or manually. Pin important items on top.

Reminders

Set one-time or recurring reminders: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually. Import dates from your calendar.

iCloud Sync

Automatic sync across all your iOS devices signed in with the same iCloud account. No setup.

List Statistics

Track progress for all lists combined or individually. See completion percentage and unchecked items.

Share and Print

Share from inside the app. Auto-screenshot your entire list and share it or print using AirPrint.

Badge Count

Red badge shows unchecked items for one list or all lists combined. Updates automatically.

Universal Search

Find any list or item instantly. Recover recently deleted items when needed.

Move Items

Move items between lists, duplicate lists, check or uncheck a whole list at once.

Create a List For Anything

Shopping and grocery lists

Add items as you think of them, check them off at the store, and share with family.

Work tasks and projects

Track deliverables, set reminders for deadlines, and watch your completion percentage climb.

Bucket lists and goals

Dream big, pin your most exciting goals on top, and check them off as you go.
