Features

Save Everything

Save subject, message body, and email addresses (To, Cc, Bcc) in reusable templates.

Two-Tap Sending

Select a template and send. The built-in mail composer opens with everything pre-filled.

Contact Integration

Import addresses from your Contacts app or type them manually. Duplicates are removed automatically.

iCloud Sync

Sync templates across all your iOS devices automatically. No setup required.

Today Widget

See your templates in Notification Center. Send an email with one tap from the widget.

Email Yourself

Quick-send a template to yourself in seconds. Perfect for reminders and notes.

Organize Templates

Edit, reorder, duplicate, and delete templates. Keep your most-used ones on top.

Built-in Composer

Emails are sent from the native mail composer, so they appear in your Sent folder.
