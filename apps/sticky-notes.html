Features

20 Beautiful Colors

Color-code your notes and filter by color. Personalize each note with multiple theme colors.

Full Customization

Sync all your sticky notes across all your devices automatically. No setup required.

Today Widget

Set alarms for your notes with repeat functionality. Import dates from your phone calendar.

Share via mail, messages, or social networks. Create note pics with backgrounds and filters for Instagram, Facebook, or Twitter.
