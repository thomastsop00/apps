Features

Full Screen Countdown

Watch the countdown full screen. Put your device on the dock and use it as a digital countdown clock.

Sync all your events across all your iOS devices automatically. No setup required.

Today Widget

See the days left at a glance in Notification Center. Navigate through events and open the app from the widget.
