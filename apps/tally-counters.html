Features

Large Buttons

Big, easy-to-tap buttons to increase and decrease counters. No limit to counter values.

Custom Step Values

Set step up, step down, and reset values for each counter. Count by 1, 5, 10, or any number.

Two Themes

Switch between dark and light mode. Set a background image for each counter.

iCloud Sync

Sync counters across all your iOS devices automatically. No setup required.

Today Widget

See counters in Notification Center. Count up, down, and reset right from the widget.

Badge Count

Show a counter's value as the app badge icon. Updates automatically.

Share Counters

Share from inside the app. Create counter pics with backgrounds and filters for social networks.

Master Reset

Reset all counters at once with one button. Or reset them individually.

Sort and Search

Sort counters manually. Instant search to find any counter. Full landscape support.

Count Anything

Fitness tracking

Count reps, sets, laps, or gym visits. Use custom step values to track weight or distance increments.

Inventory and stock

Track items in stock, count coins in a collection, or log how many products you've sold.

Habit tracking

Count how many cigarettes you had, cups of coffee, glasses of water, or any habit you want to monitor.
