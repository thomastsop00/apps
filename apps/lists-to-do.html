Features

Intuitive Design

150+ category icons. Switch between light, dark, and dark blue mode. Stunning graphics make capturing ideas easy.

Smart Organisation

Sort alphabetically, by date, or manually. Duplicate lists, move items between lists, pin favourites on top.

Reminders

Set one-time or recurring reminders: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually. Import dates from your calendar.

iCloud Sync

Automatic sync across all your iOS devices signed in with the same iCloud account. No setup required.

Share & Print

Share from inside the app. Auto-screenshot your entire list and share it or set it as a lock-screen background. Print using AirPrint.

List Statistics

Track progress for all lists combined or individually. See percentage complete, unchecked items, and more. Hit that 100%.

Badge Count

Red badge icon shows unchecked items for one list or all lists combined. Updates automatically every time you check a task.

Universal Search

Find any list or item instantly. Manage recently deleted items to recover them when needed.

Upcoming Feed

View all upcoming items based on your reminders, for all lists combined, in one feed.

Create a List For Anything

Shopping and grocery lists

Add items as you think of them, check them off at the store, and share with family so everyone knows what to grab.

Work tasks and project checklists

Track deliverables, set reminders for deadlines, and watch your completion percentage climb toward 100%.

Bucket lists and personal goals

Dream big, pin your most exciting goals on top, and check them off as you go. The statistics keep you motivated.
